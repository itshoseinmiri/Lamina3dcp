## About Lamina

Lamina is a 3D printing and additive manufacturing company specializing in transforming ideas into physical products through advanced manufacturing technologies, rapid prototyping, custom production, and engineering-driven workflows.

Our mission is to help businesses, startups, engineers, designers, and manufacturers accelerate product development and production using modern additive manufacturing solutions.
