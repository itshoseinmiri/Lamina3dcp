# Design Iteration

> Refine your product faster by printing, testing, and improving designs in rapid cycles.

## Problem / Scenario

Great products are rarely right on the first try. Each design revision traditionally means new tooling, long lead times, and mounting costs, which pressures teams to lock in decisions before they have enough evidence. This discourages experimentation and often leaves flaws undiscovered until late in development, when changes are most expensive to make.

## How Lamina helps

Lamina 3DCP makes iteration affordable and fast. Because additive manufacturing builds directly from your CAD data, every revision can be printed without new tooling. You can test one version, adjust the model, and have the next iteration in hand quickly. Our engineers provide feedback on manufacturability and material behavior between rounds, helping you converge on a strong design with fewer wasted cycles.

## Typical workflow

1. **Submit current design** — Provide the CAD model for the version you want to evaluate.
2. **Print the iteration** — We manufacture the part using the appropriate additive process.
3. **Test & evaluate** — You assess form, fit, and function against your criteria.
4. **Capture feedback** — Note what works and what needs to change; our team can advise on adjustments.
5. **Revise the model** — Update the CAD file based on findings.
6. **Repeat** — Loop through as many cycles as needed until the design is validated.

## Benefits

- Test multiple design variants without tooling costs.
- Discover and fix issues early, when changes are cheap.
- Compress development timelines with quick turnarounds.
- Encourage experimentation and creative problem-solving.
- Reach a production-ready design with confidence.
