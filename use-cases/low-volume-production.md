# Low-Volume Production

> Manufacture small batches of production-quality parts without the cost of tooling.

## Problem / Scenario

Not every product needs thousands of units. Startups launching a first product, companies serving niche markets, and teams bridging the gap between prototype and mass production all face the same obstacle: traditional manufacturing demands expensive tooling that only makes sense at high volumes. Producing tens or hundreds of parts through conventional methods is often uneconomical and slow.

## How Lamina helps

Lamina 3DCP delivers low-volume manufacturing through additive processes that require no tooling. This makes small production runs cost-effective and fast, whether you need a limited product launch, replacement inventory, or a bridge batch while production tooling is being finalized. We support consistent quality across the run and can adjust designs between batches as your product evolves.

## Benefits

- No tooling costs, making small runs economical.
- Fast production without long lead times.
- Flexibility to change designs between batches.
- Ideal for product launches, niche markets, and bridge production.
- Scales up or down to match real demand.
