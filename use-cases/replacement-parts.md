# Replacement Parts

> Recreate discontinued, obsolete, or hard-to-source components on demand.

## Problem / Scenario

When a critical component breaks and the original is discontinued, out of stock, or no longer supported, equipment can sit idle for weeks or months. Legacy machines, specialized industrial equipment, and older products often rely on parts that manufacturers no longer produce. Waiting for a rare part, or replacing an entire assembly because one piece failed, is costly and disruptive.

## How Lamina helps

Lamina 3DCP can manufacture replacement parts on demand using additive manufacturing. Working from an existing CAD model, a sample, or detailed measurements, we reproduce components without the need for original tooling. This restores obsolete and hard-to-source parts quickly, keeping equipment running and extending the life of valuable machinery across industrial, automotive, and other sectors.

## Typical workflow

1. **Identify the part** — Provide a CAD file, a physical sample, or detailed dimensions.
2. **Reconstruct the design** — If needed, our team helps develop a manufacturable model. <!-- TODO: confirm reverse-engineering / scanning capability -->
3. **Material selection** — We choose a material that matches the original part's function.
4. **Manufacturing** — The replacement part is printed to specification.
5. **Finishing & inspection** — Parts are post-processed and checked for fit.
6. **Delivery** — You receive the replacement, with the option to reorder as needed.

## Benefits

- Restore obsolete and discontinued parts.
- Avoid replacing whole assemblies over a single component.
- Minimize equipment downtime.
- No original tooling required.
- Reorder on demand instead of holding large inventories.
