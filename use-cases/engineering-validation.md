# Engineering Validation

> Prove out form, fit, and function with physical parts before committing to production.

## Problem / Scenario

Simulation and CAD analysis can only take a design so far. Before investing in production tooling or full-scale manufacturing, engineering teams need to validate that a part performs as intended under real conditions: that it assembles correctly, withstands expected loads, and meets tolerance requirements. Skipping this step risks costly failures discovered only after production has begun.

## How Lamina helps

Lamina 3DCP produces functional parts that let you validate designs in the physical world. Using additive manufacturing with engineering-grade materials, we deliver components suitable for fit checks, assembly trials, and functional testing. Our engineering consultation helps you choose materials and processes that reflect the behavior of the intended production part, so your validation results are meaningful and reliable.

## Typical workflow

1. **Define validation goals** — Tell us what you need to prove: fit, mechanical performance, thermal behavior, or assembly.
2. **Material & process selection** — We recommend options that approximate your production part's properties.
3. **Manufacture test parts** — Components are printed to your specifications and tolerances.
4. **Inspection** — Parts are checked dimensionally before delivery. <!-- TODO: confirm inspection/QA capabilities -->
5. **Testing** — You run fit checks, assembly trials, or functional tests.
6. **Iterate or approve** — Refine the design if needed, or move forward to production with confidence.

## Benefits

- Validate real-world performance before production commitment.
- Catch assembly and tolerance issues early.
- Reduce the risk of costly late-stage design changes.
- Access engineering-grade materials for meaningful results.
- Make production decisions backed by physical evidence.
