# Prototype Development

> Turning early-stage ideas into physical, testable prototypes with fast additive manufacturing.

## Problem / Scenario

Teams with a promising concept often struggle to bridge the gap between CAD models and a tangible object they can hold, test, and show to stakeholders. Traditional tooling-based prototyping is slow and expensive, and waiting weeks for a single part stalls decision-making. Product developers, engineers, and startups need physical prototypes quickly to validate form, fit, and function before committing to production.

## How Lamina helps

Lamina 3DCP specializes in transforming digital designs into manufactured reality. Using additive manufacturing, we produce accurate prototypes directly from your CAD files without the cost or lead time of traditional tooling. Our team provides engineering consultation to select the right process and material for each prototype's purpose, whether it is a visual model or a functional part meant to endure real-world testing.

## Typical workflow

1. **Share your concept** — Send us a CAD file or design brief along with your goals for the prototype.
2. **Design review** — Our engineers assess printability, material options, and tolerances, flagging any issues early.
3. **Process selection** — We recommend the additive process best suited to your requirements for detail, strength, and finish.
4. **Printing** — Your prototype is manufactured, typically within a short turnaround window. <!-- TODO: confirm typical lead time -->
5. **Finishing & inspection** — Parts are post-processed and checked against your specifications.
6. **Delivery & feedback** — You receive the prototype and can request iterations based on testing.

## Benefits

- Rapid turnaround from digital file to physical part.
- Lower upfront cost with no tooling investment.
- Freedom to test complex geometries not feasible with conventional methods.
- Expert guidance on materials and process selection.
- A faster path from idea to informed decisions.
