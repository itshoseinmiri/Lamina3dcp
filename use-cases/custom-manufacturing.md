# Custom Manufacturing

> Produce bespoke parts tailored to your exact specifications, geometry, and application.

## Problem / Scenario

Off-the-shelf components rarely fit specialized applications. Engineers and product teams often need parts with custom geometries, specific material properties, or dimensions that no catalog offers. Manufacturing these through conventional methods means custom tooling and long lead times, which is impractical for one-off or highly specialized parts.

## How Lamina helps

Lamina 3DCP is built for custom part production. Additive manufacturing lets us build almost any geometry directly from your design, including complex internal features and organic shapes that traditional processes cannot achieve. We work across industries from automotive and aerospace to healthcare and consumer products, providing engineering consultation to optimize each part for its intended use, material, and manufacturing method.

## Typical workflow

1. **Discuss requirements** — Share your application, design intent, and any constraints.
2. **Design support** — Our engineers help refine or optimize the design for additive manufacturing.
3. **Material selection** — We match the material to your part's functional and environmental needs.
4. **Manufacturing** — Your custom part is printed to specification.
5. **Finishing & inspection** — Parts are post-processed and verified against requirements.
6. **Delivery** — You receive your bespoke part, ready for use or further iteration.

## Benefits

- Build geometries impossible with traditional manufacturing.
- Tailor material and finish to the application.
- No tooling required, even for one-off parts.
- Engineering support to optimize design and manufacturability.
- Serves a wide range of industries and use cases.
