# Industrial 3D Printing

> Production-oriented 3D printing for demanding, repeatable applications where consistency matters.

## What it is

Industrial 3D printing applies additive manufacturing at a level of quality and repeatability suited to real-world use — not just prototypes. Lamina 3DCP produces functional and end-use parts built to hold up under mechanical, thermal, and environmental demands, with attention to process control and part-to-part consistency.

## Benefits

- **Repeatability** — Consistent results across parts and repeat orders.
- **Durability** — Engineering-grade materials for functional, load-bearing use.
- **Complexity without penalty** — Produce intricate geometries, internal channels, and consolidated assemblies in a single part.
- **On-demand supply** — Print parts when needed, reducing inventory and tooling dependence.

## Typical applications

- End-use functional components
- Replacement and spare parts
- Manufacturing aids: jigs, fixtures, and tooling
- Bracketry, housings, and enclosures
- Bridge production between prototype and full-scale manufacturing

## Materials

Engineering plastics in production-grade formulations, selected for strength, temperature resistance, and dimensional stability appropriate to the application.
