# Lamina 3DCP Products & Services Overview

> Additive manufacturing and 3D printing services that turn ideas into manufactured reality.

## What it is

Lamina 3DCP is an additive manufacturing and 3D printing service provider. We help teams move from concept to physical part, covering the full path from early prototypes through low-volume production. Our capabilities span rapid iteration, functional prototyping, custom part production, and end-to-end product development support, backed by engineering consultation and manufacturing optimization.

Website: [lamina3dcp.com](https://lamina3dcp.com)

## Core services

- **[Rapid Prototyping](./rapid-prototyping.md)** — Fast, iterative physical prototypes to validate form, fit, and function.
- **[Industrial 3D Printing](./industrial-3d-printing.md)** — Production-oriented 3D printing for demanding, repeatable applications.
- **[Additive Manufacturing](./additive-manufacturing.md)** — Building parts layer by layer with design freedom conventional methods can't match.
- **[Custom Part Production](./custom-parts.md)** — Made-to-spec parts and components tailored to your requirements.
- **[Manufacturing Services](./manufacturing-services.md)** — Product development support, engineering consultation, low-volume manufacturing, and manufacturing optimization.

## Industries we serve

Automotive, Aerospace, Architecture, Industrial Equipment, Consumer Products, Electronics, Healthcare, and Startups.
