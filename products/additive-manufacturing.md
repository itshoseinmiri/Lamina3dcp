# Additive Manufacturing

> Building parts layer by layer to unlock design freedom conventional methods can't match.

## What it is

Additive manufacturing (AM) creates parts by adding material layer by layer directly from a digital model, rather than cutting away from a block or forming with tooling. This approach removes many constraints of traditional manufacturing, enabling complex geometries, internal features, and part consolidation. It is the foundation of everything Lamina 3DCP does, from prototypes to production parts.

## Benefits

- **Geometric freedom** — Produce shapes, lattices, and internal channels that are difficult or impossible to machine or mold.
- **Part consolidation** — Combine multiple components into one, reducing assembly and failure points.
- **No tooling** — Skip molds and dies, lowering cost and lead time for low volumes.
- **Design-driven** — Optimize parts for performance and weight rather than for manufacturability alone.

## Typical applications

- Lightweight and topology-optimized structures
- Consolidated assemblies
- Complex enclosures, ducts, and manifolds
- Custom and made-to-order components
- Prototype-through-production workflows
