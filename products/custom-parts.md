# Custom Part Production

> Made-to-spec parts and components tailored precisely to your requirements.

## What it is

Custom part production turns your specifications into finished physical parts using additive manufacturing. Whether you need a one-off component, a replacement for an obsolete part, or a small batch built to exact requirements, Lamina 3DCP produces parts matched to your geometry, material, and functional needs — without the cost and delay of dedicated tooling.

## Benefits

- **Made to spec** — Parts built to your design, dimensions, and performance requirements.
- **No tooling investment** — Ideal for unique or low-quantity parts.
- **Fast turnaround** — Go from design file to finished part quickly.
- **Flexibility** — Change designs between orders without retooling.

## Typical applications

- Bespoke components and one-off parts
- Replacement and legacy/obsolete parts
- Custom brackets, housings, and enclosures
- Specialized jigs, fixtures, and tooling aids
- Short-run custom assemblies
