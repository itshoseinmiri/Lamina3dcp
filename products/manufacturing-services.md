# Manufacturing Services

> Product development support, engineering consultation, low-volume manufacturing, and manufacturing optimization — from idea to finished part.

## What it is

Beyond printing parts, Lamina 3DCP offers end-to-end manufacturing services that support the whole journey from concept to production. This includes product development support, engineering consultation, functional prototypes, low-volume manufacturing, and manufacturing optimization — helping teams design better parts and produce them more efficiently.

## What's included

- **Product development support** — Guidance from concept through to a production-ready part.
- **Engineering consultation** — Design-for-additive-manufacturing advice on material selection, geometry, and process fit.
- **Functional prototypes** — Working prototypes that stand in for final parts during testing.
- **Low-volume manufacturing** — Small production runs without the cost of dedicated tooling.
- **Manufacturing optimization** — Refining designs and processes to improve quality, cost, and throughput.

## Benefits

- **One partner, full path** — Design help and production under one roof.
- **Lower barrier to production** — Reach small runs without tooling investment.
- **Better parts** — Additive-aware design decisions made early.
- **Scalable** — Move from prototype to low-volume production smoothly.

## Typical applications

- Bringing a new product from concept to first production run
- Design reviews and DfAM optimization
- Bridge and low-volume production
- Iterative functional testing programs

## Materials

Engineering plastics across functional, prototype, and production-grade options, matched to each project's requirements.
