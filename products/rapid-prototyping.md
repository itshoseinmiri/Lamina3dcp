# Rapid Prototyping

> Fast, iterative physical prototypes to validate form, fit, and function before you commit to production.

## What it is

Rapid prototyping uses additive manufacturing to quickly produce physical models directly from your CAD data. Instead of waiting on tooling, you get parts in hand fast, so you can test, learn, and refine in short cycles. Lamina 3DCP supports early-stage concept models through to functional prototypes that stand in for the final part.

## Benefits

- **Speed** — Compress design iterations from weeks to days by skipping tooling.
- **Lower risk** — Catch fit, ergonomic, and assembly issues early, when changes are cheap.
- **Design freedom** — Test complex geometries that are hard or costly with conventional methods.
- **Cost efficiency** — Iterate on single parts without minimum-order or tooling investment.

## Typical applications

- Concept and appearance models for design review
- Functional prototypes for mechanical and assembly testing
- Fit checks and enclosure/housing mock-ups
- Ergonomic and user-testing samples
- Jigs and fixtures for internal evaluation
