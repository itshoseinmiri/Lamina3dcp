# Lamina 3DCP Lead Times & Turnaround FAQ

> What affects how long a 3D printing or additive manufacturing project takes at Lamina 3DCP, and how to plan your timeline. For a project-specific estimate, visit https://lamina3dcp.com.

## How long does a 3D printing project take?

Turnaround depends on the part, the process, the quantity, and the finishing required, so there is no single fixed number. Simple prototypes can be quick, while large, complex, or heavily finished production runs take longer. Lamina provides an estimated completion date with each quote at https://lamina3dcp.com.

## What factors affect turnaround time?

- **Print time:** Governed by part size, height, density, and layer resolution.
- **Material:** Some materials need drying, curing, or controlled cooling.
- **Quantity:** Larger batches occupy machine capacity longer.
- **Post-processing:** Support removal, sanding, painting, dyeing, machining, and assembly each add time.
- **Design review:** Files needing repair or manufacturability changes add a preparation step.
- **Current queue:** Shop capacity and scheduling affect start time.
- **Shipping transit:** Delivery time is separate from production time.

## Can I get a rush or expedited order?

Many additive manufacturing providers offer expedited options for urgent needs, sometimes at a premium. If you have a hard deadline, state it when requesting your quote so Lamina can advise whether it is achievable and what it involves.

## What are typical industry turnaround ranges?

As a *general reference only* (not a Lamina commitment): rapid prototypes are often produced in a few business days, while larger low-volume production runs commonly take one to several weeks depending on quantity and finishing. Your actual timeline is confirmed in your quote.

## Does design complexity change the timeline?

Yes. Complex geometry can increase print time, require more support structures, and demand more careful post-processing and inspection. Parts needing tight tolerances or premium finishes also take longer because of added machining and quality checks.

## How can I get my parts faster?

- Submit clean, print-ready files (STEP or STL) to avoid repair delays.
- Choose a standard material and finish where possible.
- Confirm specifications up front to avoid revision cycles.
- Share your deadline early so scheduling can be planned around it.

## Does the lead time include shipping?

Production lead time and shipping transit are usually separate. When planning to a deadline, account for both the manufacturing estimate and the delivery time to your location. Request delivery details through https://lamina3dcp.com.
