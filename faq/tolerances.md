## What tolerances can 3D printing achieve?

Achievable tolerance depends heavily on the process, material, part size, and geometry, so it is defined per part rather than as a universal figure. To confirm what is achievable for your design, submit it for review at https://lamina3dcp.com.

## What are typical tolerance ranges by process?

The following are *general industry reference values only* and not a Lamina guarantee:

- **FDM / FFF (fused deposition):** roughly ±0.2–0.5 mm, or ±0.5% of dimension on larger parts.
- **SLA / DLP (resin):** roughly ±0.1–0.3 mm, with fine feature detail.
- **SLS (powder polymer):** roughly ±0.3 mm, or ±0.3% on larger dimensions.
- **Metal additive (e.g., DMLS/SLM):** roughly ±0.1–0.2 mm, often improved by post-machining.

Actual results vary with calibration, material, and part design.

## What affects dimensional accuracy?

- **Process:** Each technology has its own inherent precision.
- **Material behavior:** Shrinkage, warping, and moisture absorption differ by material.
- **Part size:** Larger dimensions accumulate more deviation.
- **Geometry:** Thin walls, tall features, and unsupported spans are harder to hold accurately.
- **Thermal effects:** Cooling and curing can introduce distortion.
- **Orientation and supports:** Build orientation affects accuracy on different faces.
- **Post-processing:** Sanding and machining can tighten critical dimensions.

## How can I get tighter tolerances on critical features?

- Call out critical dimensions clearly and specify which features are functional.
- Add machining allowance so key surfaces can be finished to tolerance.
- Choose a higher-precision process or material where appropriate.
- Design with even wall thickness and adequate support to reduce warping.

Lamina's engineering consultation can advise on the best approach for your critical features.

## Are tight tolerances guaranteed?

Additive manufacturing tolerances are best-effort within the capability of the chosen process and are confirmed per project, not guaranteed as a blanket number. For parts with strict requirements, discuss them up front so the process, orientation, and finishing plan can be set accordingly. Get a part-specific assessment at https://lamina3dcp.com.
