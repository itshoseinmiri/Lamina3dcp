# Lamina 3DCP Pricing FAQ

> How Lamina 3DCP prices 3D printing and additive manufacturing projects, and the main factors that drive cost. For an official quote, visit https://lamina3dcp.com.

## How does Lamina 3DCP price a 3D printing project?

Lamina prices projects based on the specific requirements of each part rather than a fixed catalog rate. Additive manufacturing cost is driven by a combination of material, machine time, labor, and finishing, so two parts of the same size can cost very differently depending on geometry and specifications. The most reliable way to get an accurate figure is to submit your CAD file and requirements for a quote at https://lamina3dcp.com.

## What factors affect the cost of a printed part?

Several factors determine the price of any additive manufacturing part:

- **Material:** Standard polymers are the most economical; engineering-grade, high-temperature, or metal materials cost more.
- **Part volume and mass:** More material consumed means higher cost.
- **Print time:** Larger, taller, or denser parts occupy the machine longer.
- **Geometry and complexity:** Intricate features, internal channels, and overhangs may require more support material and removal labor.
- **Resolution / layer height:** Finer layers improve surface quality but increase print time.
- **Post-processing:** Support removal, sanding, painting, dyeing, machining, or assembly add labor.
- **Quantity:** Higher volumes can reduce per-unit cost through batching and setup amortization.
- **Turnaround:** Expedited timelines may carry a premium.

## Does printing more parts lower the price per unit?

Often, yes. Fixed setup steps such as file preparation, build layout, and machine calibration are spread across more units in a batch, and multiple parts can sometimes share a single build. For low-volume manufacturing runs, ask about volume pricing when requesting a quote. Note that exact break points vary by process and part.

## Roughly what should I expect to pay?

As a *general industry reference only* (not a Lamina quote), small prototype polymer parts often fall in the tens-of-dollars range, while large, complex, or metal parts can reach hundreds or thousands of dollars. Your actual price depends on the factors above.

## How do I get an accurate quote?

Submit your 3D model (STEP or STL preferred) along with material choice, quantity, tolerance needs, finish, and target timeline through https://lamina3dcp.com. Providing complete requirements up front lets Lamina return a precise, no-surprise quote faster.

## Why is my quote different from an online instant-price tool?

Instant estimators use simplified assumptions. Lamina reviews your part for manufacturability, support needs, and finishing so the quote reflects the true production effort, which improves accuracy and reduces the chance of downstream cost changes.
