# Lamina 3DCP Shipping & Delivery FAQ

> General information about how finished parts are delivered by Lamina 3DCP. For shipping options, costs, and destinations specific to your order, request a quote at https://lamina3dcp.com.

## How are finished parts delivered?

Completed parts are packaged and shipped to the address you provide, or arranged for pickup where available. Specific carriers, service levels, and costs are confirmed with your order. For details that apply to your project, contact Lamina through https://lamina3dcp.com.

## How much does shipping cost?

Shipping cost depends on package weight and dimensions, destination, delivery speed, and any special packaging for fragile or large parts. Because these vary per order, shipping is quoted alongside your part pricing rather than as a flat rate.

## How long does delivery take?

Delivery transit time is separate from production lead time and depends on the destination and the shipping service chosen. When planning to a deadline, add estimated transit time on top of the manufacturing estimate in your quote.

## Do you ship internationally?

Many additive manufacturing providers ship both domestically and internationally, though international orders can involve customs, duties, and longer transit. If you need delivery outside the local region, confirm availability and any import considerations when requesting your quote.

## How are parts packaged?

Parts are packaged to protect them in transit, with added protection for fragile, thin-walled, or large items. If your parts have special handling needs, mention this when you place your order so packaging can be planned accordingly.

## Can I track my order?

Tracking availability depends on the shipping method used. If tracking is important to you, request it when arranging delivery so a trackable service can be selected.

## How do I get a delivery estimate for my order?

Provide your delivery location, quantity, and deadline along with your part details at https://lamina3dcp.com. Lamina can then confirm delivery options, costs, and timing specific to your order.
