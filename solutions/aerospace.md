# Additive Manufacturing for Aerospace

> How Lamina supports aerospace teams with rapid prototyping and additive manufacturing for lightweight, complex, low-volume parts.

## Industry challenges

Aerospace programs demand lightweight parts, complex geometries, and rigorous validation — often in small quantities. Development timelines are long, and every gram matters. Conventional manufacturing of intricate ducting, brackets, and housings can require costly tooling and extended lead times, while legacy and low-volume components are difficult to source economically.

## How additive manufacturing helps

Additive manufacturing excels at the geometric complexity and weight optimization aerospace values. Topology-optimized brackets, internal cooling and airflow channels, and consolidated assemblies reduce part count and mass. Tool-free production shortens lead times for prototypes and low-volume runs, and supports on-demand manufacturing of specialty or hard-to-source parts.

## Lamina services for this industry

- **Rapid Prototyping** — fast validation of form and fit for design reviews.
- **Functional Prototypes** — parts for mechanical and airflow testing.
- **Low-Volume Manufacturing** — short-run and bridge production without tooling.
- **Custom Part Production** — brackets, ducts, housings, and fixtures.
- **Engineering Consultation** — design-for-additive and lightweighting support.

## Example applications

- Lightweight, topology-optimized brackets and mounts
- Air and fluid ducting with internal channels
- Housings and enclosures for avionics and instrumentation
- Ground-support and assembly tooling
- Low-volume and legacy replacement parts

## Materials & considerations

Aerospace parts often require materials selected for strength-to-weight, temperature resistance, and flammability behavior. Match material and process to the loads, environment, and service life the part will experience.
