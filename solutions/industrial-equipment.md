# Additive Manufacturing for Industrial Equipment

> How Lamina helps equipment manufacturers prototype, produce custom parts, and keep machines running with additive manufacturing.

## Industry challenges

Industrial equipment makers balance robust performance with the need to iterate on new designs and support existing machines in the field. Custom fixtures, jigs, and replacement parts are often needed in small quantities, where tooling costs and lead times are hard to justify. Discontinued or legacy components can be difficult and expensive to source, causing costly downtime.

## How additive manufacturing helps

Additive manufacturing produces functional parts and tooling on demand, without minimum-order molds. It shortens the design-iterate-test loop for new equipment and enables geometries — consolidated assemblies, internal channels, ergonomic tooling — that reduce part count and weight. On-demand production of spares and legacy parts helps minimize downtime and inventory.

- Jigs, fixtures, and assembly aids
- Custom housings, guards, and enclosures
- Ergonomic tool grips and handling components
- Replacement and legacy spare parts
- End-of-arm tooling and machine accessories

## Materials & considerations

Match material to the mechanical load, wear, temperature, and chemical exposure each part will face. Engineering polymers cover many structural and tooling needs, with more specialized grades for heat or chemical resistance. Plan tolerances, fit, and surface finish for parts that mate with existing assemblies. For production spares, confirm expected duty cycle so the process suits real service conditions.
