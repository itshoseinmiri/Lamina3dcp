# Additive Manufacturing for Consumer Products

> How Lamina helps product teams and startups move from idea to market-ready design with rapid prototyping and low-volume production.

## Industry challenges

Consumer product development lives or dies on speed and iteration. Teams need to test look, feel, and function quickly, refine based on feedback, and validate a design before committing to mass-production tooling. Traditional prototyping is slow, and injection-mold tooling is a large up-front cost that is risky before a design is proven. Small brands and startups often need short runs, not thousands of units.

## How additive manufacturing helps

Additive manufacturing enables fast, low-cost iteration on form and function, so teams can hold and test real parts within days. It supports realistic functional prototypes for user testing and validation, and enables low-volume and bridge production before or instead of hard tooling. Custom and personalized products become economical at small quantities.

## Lamina services for this industry

- **Rapid Prototyping** — quick concept and appearance models.
- **Functional Prototypes** — parts for real-world user and mechanical testing.
- **Product Development Support** — iteration from concept to production-ready.
- **Low-Volume Manufacturing** — bridge and short-run production.

## Example applications

- Concept and appearance models for design reviews
- Functional prototypes of housings and enclosures
- Ergonomic grips, fittings, and accessories
- Short-run and bridge production before tooling
- Custom and personalized consumer goods

## Materials & considerations

Choose materials by the product's purpose — appearance models, functional testing, or end-use. Consider surface finish, color, texture, durability, and whether parts will be painted or assembled. Fit and tolerances matter for multi-part assemblies. As volumes grow, discuss whether additive remains the best fit or whether a transition to molding makes sense.
