# Additive Manufacturing for Healthcare

> How Lamina supports healthcare and medical-device teams with prototyping and low-volume additive manufacturing for research and development.

## Industry challenges

Healthcare and medical-device development involves careful iteration, validation, and often highly customized or patient-specific geometry. Teams need accurate physical parts for design reviews, ergonomic evaluation, and functional testing, frequently in small quantities. Custom fixtures, models, and enclosures are difficult and slow to produce with conventional methods, which can extend development timelines.

## How additive manufacturing helps

Additive manufacturing turns detailed digital models into accurate physical parts quickly, supporting design iteration and functional prototyping. It enables complex, customized, and low-volume geometries — anatomical and visualization models, ergonomic device housings, and testing fixtures — without dedicated tooling. Faster iteration helps research and development teams evaluate concepts earlier.

## Lamina services for this industry

- **Rapid Prototyping** — quick-turn models for design and ergonomic review.
- **Functional Prototypes** — parts for fit, form, and mechanical evaluation.
- **Product Development Support** — iteration on device concepts.
- **Custom Part Production** — fixtures, housings, and specialized components.

## Example applications

- Anatomical and visualization models for planning and education
- Prototype device housings and enclosures
- Ergonomic evaluation and fit-check parts
- Lab and research fixtures and jigs
- Low-volume custom components for development

## Materials & considerations

Material selection depends on the intended use, handling, and environment of each part. Many applications here are for prototyping, visualization, and research rather than clinical use.
