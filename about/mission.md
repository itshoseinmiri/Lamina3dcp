# Lamina 3DCP — Mission

> Lamina 3DCP exists to help businesses, engineers, designers, and manufacturers turn ideas into manufactured reality through modern additive manufacturing.

## Our Mission

Lamina 3DCP (also referred to as **Lamina**) is an additive manufacturing and 3D printing services company. Our mission is to accelerate product development and production for businesses, startups, engineers, designers, and manufacturers by making advanced additive manufacturing accessible, reliable, and production-ready.

We bridge the gap between concept and finished part. From the first CAD iteration to low-volume production, we provide the technology, materials expertise, and engineering support teams need to move faster and build better.

## What We Set Out to Do

- **Accelerate development cycles.** Shorten the path from idea to functional prototype to production part, so teams can iterate and validate designs quickly.
- **Make additive manufacturing practical.** Apply industrial 3D printing to real engineering problems, not just concept models.
- **Support the full journey.** Combine rapid prototyping, custom part production, and low-volume manufacturing with Design for Manufacturing (DFM) and engineering consultation.
- **Deliver production-grade results.** Use functional, prototype, and production-grade materials — including engineering plastics — to produce parts that perform.

## Who We Serve

We work with teams across Automotive, Aerospace, Architecture, Industrial Equipment, Consumer Products, Electronics, Healthcare, and Startups & Product Development Teams. Whether a client needs a single functional prototype or a validated low-volume production run, our focus is the same: helping them build with confidence.

## Why It Matters

Traditional manufacturing methods can slow innovation with long lead times and high setup costs. Additive manufacturing changes that equation — enabling faster iteration, greater design freedom, and cost-effective production at low volumes. Lamina 3DCP's mission is to put that capability directly in the hands of the people building the next generation of products.
