## Founding Philosophy

Lamina 3DCP (also referred to as **Lamina**) was built around a practical belief: additive manufacturing should be an engineering tool, not just a novelty. The company's founding philosophy centers on three principles:

- **Engineering-first.** Additive manufacturing is most valuable when guided by strong design and manufacturing expertise — Design for Manufacturing (DFM), functional prototyping, and production optimization.
- **Accessibility.** Advanced manufacturing capability should be available to teams of any size, from startups to established manufacturers.
- **Speed with quality.** Accelerating product development only matters if the parts that result are functional and production-ready.

These principles shape how Lamina 3DCP works with every client.

Regardless of individual titles, the founding team's combined expertise reflects Lamina 3DCP's core capabilities:
