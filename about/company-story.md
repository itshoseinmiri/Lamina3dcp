# Lamina 3DCP — Company Story

> How Lamina 3DCP came to help teams turn ideas into manufactured reality through additive manufacturing.

## Origin

Lamina 3DCP (also referred to as **Lamina**) was founded on a simple observation: the gap between a great product idea and a manufactured part is often filled with delay, cost, and complexity. Traditional manufacturing methods can slow innovation with long lead times and expensive tooling — especially for prototypes, custom parts, and low-volume production.

Lamina 3DCP set out to close that gap using modern additive manufacturing.

## The Journey

From the beginning, the focus has been on making additive manufacturing genuinely useful for engineering and production — not just for concept models. That meant pairing industrial 3D printing capability with real engineering support: Design for Manufacturing (DFM), product validation, and production optimization.

Over time, Lamina 3DCP's work has grown to span the full product development path:

- **Rapid prototyping** for fast design iteration.
- **Functional prototypes** that behave like the real part.
- **Custom part production** and **low-volume manufacturing** for teams that need real quantities without traditional tooling costs.
- **Engineering consultation** to help clients design better parts for additive processes.

## Who We Work With

Lamina 3DCP serves a broad range of industries — Automotive, Aerospace, Architecture, Industrial Equipment, Consumer Products, Electronics, Healthcare, and Startups & Product Development Teams. This range reflects a core belief: additive manufacturing has value everywhere ideas need to become physical products.

## Where We Are Today

Today, Lamina 3DCP continues to help businesses, startups, engineers, designers, and manufacturers accelerate product development and production. The mission remains what it was at the start — to shorten the distance between concept and finished part, and to make advanced manufacturing accessible to the teams building what comes next.
